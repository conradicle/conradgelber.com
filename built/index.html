miamidadecommissionwatch.com

HTML and JavaScript, Vercel, Supabase, Cloudflare

The public site for Miami-Dade Commission Watch. It carries plain-language summaries of every county commission meeting in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole, and a directory of all 13 commissioners by district. I also set up all of the project's email infrastructure. Residents use it to follow what the Board of County Commissioners decided without reading the agenda themselves.

2026

MDCW district lookup

JavaScript, Census Bureau geocoder, Vercel function

Residents enter an address and get back their county commission district and commissioner. The address goes through a small serverless proxy to the Census Bureau geocoder, and the browser matches the point against the county's district boundaries. It answers the first question most people have before public comment: who represents me?
