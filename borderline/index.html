Borderline

Five old political maps with the date torn off. Each shows only borders and country names. Work out when it was printed: a Russian Alaska, a Persia not yet called Iran, an Austria-Hungary still in one piece. Every map fits a window of years. A guess inside it scores 5,000; the further outside, the fewer points.

    About Borderline
    
      
    The idea came from a map in an IB Theory of Knowledge classroom that
        still shows Russian Alaska. We tried to date it from its borders and
        names alone.
    
      
    Every map is drawn in the same old-atlas style whatever its year,
        so the style never gives the date away. A map fits a window of
        years: from the latest change among the things on it to the day
        before the next change. A border change counts only if it would be
        visible on a phone, a patch of at least 120 square pixels that is
        more than two pixels thick; a rename counts if the country is
        labeled. The two changes that set the window are the tells.
    
      
    Borders follow the CShapes 2.0 dataset. Where territory is disputed,
        the map shows that dataset's coding, which is not a position on
        sovereignty.
    
      
    Data
    
      
      
        
    • CShapes 2.0 (1886 to 2019),
          by Guy Schvitz, Seraina Rüegger, Luc Girardin, Lars-Erik Cederman,
          Nils Weidmann and Kristian Skrede Gleditsch, ETH Zürich, from the
          cshapes R package.
          Licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0;
          the map files made from it here are shared under the same license.
      • 
        
    • CShapes-Europe (1816 to 1885),
          by Lars-Erik Cederman, Luc Girardin, Carl Müller-Crepon and Yannick Pengl,
          from Nationalism and the Transformation of the State (Cambridge
          University Press, 2025). Licensed
          CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
          Its records are the borders of 1 January each year, so each change
          used as a tell here carries its real date from a cited source.
      • 
        
    • historical-basemaps
          by André Ourednik, for the hand-picked maps before 1886 outside Europe.
          Licensed GPL-3.0;
          the map files made from it here are shared under the same license.
          Its snapshots mix borders from different years, so each crop was
          checked by hand and dated from the cited facts it shows.
      • 
        
    • Country names, renames, the dates CShapes-Europe leaves out, and the
          hand-picked maps are in the
          data folder,
          each date with its source.
