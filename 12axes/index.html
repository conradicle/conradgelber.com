12 Axes

A political values test that places you on twelve separate axes instead of a single left-right line. You'll see a series of statements and say how much you agree with each. At the end you get a score on every axis, the ideologies, thinkers, and historical countries closest to your answers, and a list showing which answers moved each score. Everything runs in your browser: your answers are not sent anywhere. About the axes

                  About the axes

                  Each axis runs between two poles. Neither end is better; they are different answers to the same question. Your score is the share of your answers that pointed toward each pole, so the two always add up to 100%.

                  How scoring works

                  Every statement leans toward one pole of its axis. Agreeing moves you toward that pole and disagreeing moves you away from it; strong answers count twice as much as plain ones, and Neutral counts for nothing. Each axis is then scaled against the most your own statements could have moved it, so a Short run and an Extreme run land on the same 0 to 100 scale. Statements are drawn at random each time, with some leaning each way on every axis.
