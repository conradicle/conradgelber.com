State Foreign Influence Observatory

A nonpartisan tracker for foreign government influence in American state and local government. It will pull together the new state foreign agent registries, mine federal FARA filings for contacts with governors, mayors, and state legislators, and make all of it searchable, with every claim linked to its source document.

Where it stands, November 2026: the plan and the first layer, a tracker of the new state laws. The registry and filing pipelines are not built yet, so nothing below is drawn from them.

The gap

Until 2025, no state had lobbying rules aimed specifically at people who represent foreign governments. Then Texas, Nebraska, Louisiana, Arkansas, and Oklahoma passed their own foreign agent registration laws, often called "baby FARA" laws, and five more states introduced bills in early 2026. Each one sets up its own registry with its own definitions and exemptions, and so far no one is collecting them in one place.

The federal backstop is getting weaker at the same time. A 2024 Justice Department advisory opinion suggested for the first time that contacts with state officials might fall outside federal FARA registration, and in 2025 the FBI's Foreign Influence Task Force, which worked with state and local authorities, was disbanded.

The federal filings that do mention state officials are hard to use. Much of the FARA record sits in scanned attachments, and the existing trackers focus on Washington. Reading those documents at scale is the part that needs AI.

What gets built

Started

Law tracker

Every state foreign agent bill, its status, and its legal challenges, with critics of the laws documented as carefully as their supporters. The table above is its first version.

Next

Federal filings, read for state and local contacts

Pull FARA registration and supplemental statements, have Claude extract every contact with a non-federal official, and spot-check the results by hand. This is the layer most likely to turn up something new, so it gets tested first.

Planned

State registry tracker

Ingest each state registry as it comes online and normalize the filings into one format. Early filings will be sparse, and who is not registering is a finding too.

Planned

Other public traces

Foreign-paid travel for state legislators, sister-city agreements and memorandums of understanding, and foreign trade office activity, wherever states require disclosure.

Planned

Search and questions

Country and state pages, plain search, and a Claude-powered way to ask "which foreign governments have lobbied Florida officials?" and get a sourced answer. An MCP server will expose the same data to other tools.

Ground rules

  • Every foreign principal is tracked the same way, allies and adversaries alike.
  • Every claim links to its source document.
  • One methodology for every country and every state.
  • Critics of the new state laws get the same care as their supporters.
  • Corrections are public. Spot an error? Email conrad@conradgelber.com.
