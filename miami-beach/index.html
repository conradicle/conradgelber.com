THE GEOGRAPHY COMES FIRST

A city model in progress.

The complete municipal boundary sets the scope. The base inventory includes every intersecting footprint in the downloaded county dataset, with streets, waterways, green spaces and mapped trees from public GIS layers.

Realism pilot

The Ocean Drive pilot imports 113 county 3D building models, NOAA aerial photography captured October 21, 2023, and a terrain surface sampled from the 2021 laser survey. Tree locations come from the city inventory; nearby vegetation returns inform their estimated heights. Real roof photographs are projected onto the models. Image shadows are baked into the photographs.

Height check: 102 of 110 matched Ocean Drive models have roof elevations within 2 m of the sampled laser roof level; the median absolute difference is 0.22 m. This compares older sources, not today’s surveyed conditions. The Diamond tower models omit upper roof details and remain flagged for review. The pilot does not establish citywide accuracy.

The Colony facade uses a GIS-aligned front edge. Its vertical sign and horizontal bands were checked against the hotel’s own exterior reference. Smaller proportions, wall colors and windows remain interpretations. The reference photograph is not redistributed.

Pilot height checks · NOAA imagery and CC0 terms · NOAA laser survey · County 3D source

Outside the pilot: pastel materials, windows and landmark forms are AI-assisted procedural artwork. Heights use plausible city floor counts × 3.2 m where matched, or a 6.4 m fallback. Ground outside the photographed pilot is flat; bridge decks are approximate. Source data includes older survey vintages and may omit recent changes.

County 2D HEIGHT values are retained in detailed records, but their units are undocumented by that service, so they are not converted into geometry. The separate 3D service explicitly declares metre geometry. This is a geographic exploration, not a surveyed replica or a flood model.

  3. Next: resolve tower crown discrepancies, validate more districts, refresh missing structures and extend licensed ground imagery.

Base data sources

    City and county geometry is provided as-is and is unsuitable for surveying, engineering or site-specific decisions. NOAA imagery is CC0-1.0. Geometry, imagery and point samples are served with this website; no Google tiles or live GIS requests.
