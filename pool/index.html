Pool

8-ball and 9-ball on a top-down table. Play the computer, or start a room and send the code to a friend.

How the rules work

Both games

  • Fouls: scratching, hitting no ball, hitting the wrong ball first, or no ball reaching a rail after contact when nothing is pocketed. A foul gives the other player ball in hand anywhere on the table.
  • Each shot has a 60-second clock. Running out is a foul.

8-ball

  • The table is open after the break. The first ball legally pocketed decides who has solids and who has stripes.
  • Pocketing a ball on the break keeps your turn. The 8 on the break goes back on the foot spot and the breaker carries on.
  • Once your group is down, call a pocket and sink the 8 to win. You lose if the 8 goes down early, in the wrong pocket, or with a scratch.

9-ball

  • Hit the lowest-numbered ball first. Pocketing any ball on a legal shot keeps your turn.
  • Legally pocketing the 9 wins at any time, even on the break or a combination. A 9 pocketed on a foul goes back on the foot spot.

Pool

Drag the slider and let go to shoot, or set it and press Shoot.

Where the tip hits the cue ball

Top is follow, bottom is draw, left and right are side spin.

Controls

Drag on the table to aim, or tap a ball to aim straight at it. With ball in hand, drag the cue ball. With the table focused: arrow keys aim, Shift and an arrow aims finely, Plus and Minus set power, W, A, S and D move the tip for spin, 0 centres it, Enter shoots. With ball in hand, press M to move the cue ball with the arrow keys, then Enter to place it. On the 8, press 1 to 6 to call a pocket.
