I pitch for the varsity baseball team at Miami Beach Senior High. I shoot trap at Markham Park in Broward County, where my best round so far is 21 of 25. And I fish the Everglades backcountry and the Biscayne Bay flats for snook, tarpon, and redfish, all of which go back in the water. I also love skiing, hiking, ping pong, and billiards.

I love to travel. So far I've been to Ecuador (including the Galápagos Islands), Iceland, the United Kingdom, Italy, Japan, Canada, Israel, Jordan, and the British Virgin Islands.

When I have ten minutes, I play one of the games I built.
