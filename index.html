Miami Beach, Florida Miami-Dade Commission Watch conrad@conradgelber.com

Most people never see a county commission meeting. The agendas run hundreds of pages, the votes happen on a Tuesday morning, and the decisions reach further into daily life than almost anything that happens in Tallahassee or Washington. In August 2026 I co-founded a nonprofit to fix that for Miami-Dade, and I have been building the coverage, the volunteer bench, and the software behind it ever since.

The rest of my work sits in the same territory: government transparency, international politics, and the question of how institutions earn the trust they ask for.

Where this is going

I want to join the Foreign Service, most likely on the political or economic track, and I intend to spend a career in it. The work I do now is the same work at a smaller scale: read the documents nobody reads, figure out what a government is actually doing rather than what it says it is doing, and write it down clearly enough that it changes what someone else decides.

If that path closes, political and geopolitical risk analysis is the version of the job that exists in the private sector, and I am building the technical skills to do it well.
