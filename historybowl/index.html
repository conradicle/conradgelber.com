    About

    Built for my Rho Kappa team's practice. The National History Bowl is run by International Academic Competitions; this page is not affiliated with them.

    Questions come live from QB Reader, a free quizbowl database, including the IAC History Bee national finals it hosts. Each question shows its source. Answers are checked with QB Reader's checker, or a simpler one built in here when that is out of reach, and you can always overrule it.
