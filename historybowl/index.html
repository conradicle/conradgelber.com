    Match simulator

    A full regional match against the computer: ten short tossups, then eight longer ones with a 10-point bonus for each you get, then the lightning round (the team behind at halftime picks the category first; 60 seconds, 8 questions, 20 extra for all 8; misses bounce to the other side), then eight long tossups worth 30, 20 or 10 by how early you buzz. No penalties for wrong answers. A tie goes to sudden-death tossups.

    How good is the computer?

    Lightning bouncebacks

    Questions follow your source and level settings. The first two quarters use the last sentences of longer tossups, marked "shortened". The fourth quarter uses the IAC Bee's full pyramids when that source is on, and counts in your stats.

      About

      Built for my Rho Kappa team's practice. The National History Bowl is run by International Academic Competitions; this page is not affiliated with them.

      Questions come live from QB Reader, a free quizbowl database, including the IAC History Bee national finals it hosts. Each question shows its source. Answers are checked with QB Reader's checker, or a simpler one built in here when that is out of reach, and you can always overrule it.
